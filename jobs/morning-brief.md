# Morning brief

Give the owner a short start to the day from their notes.

1. Read Memory.md, Now.md, and yesterday's and today's daily notes in Daily/ (skip any that don't exist).
2. Write a section at the top of today's daily note (below the heading) called `## Morning brief`:
   - **Today:** anything dated today or overdue in Now.md or yesterday's notes.
   - **Open threads:** up to 5 items from Now.md that are still open, oldest first.
   - **Yesterday:** 2-3 lines on what got done, from yesterday's daily note.
   - **One suggestion:** the single most useful thing to do first today, and why, in one line.
   If today's note already has a `## Morning brief` (a rerun), replace that section instead of adding a second one.
3. Keep it under 20 lines. If there's nothing for a part, write "nothing".
4. End with `NOTIFY: ` and the one suggestion, shortened.
